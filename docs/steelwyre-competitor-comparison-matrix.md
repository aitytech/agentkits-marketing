## Customer Research: Who Chooses Whom?

### Jasper Customers
**Profile:** Marketing manager/team at 10-50 person SaaS company
**Why Jasper:** Need consistent, on-brand content at scale; have team to execute
**Pain point HubSpot solves:** Content creation speed and consistency

### Copy.ai Customers
**Profile:** Sales leader or growth PM at 10-100 person SaaS; agency
**Why Copy.ai:** Need to automate prospecting and outreach sequences
**Pain point Copy.ai solves:** Sales/GTM workflow automation

### HubSpot Customers
**Profile:** Marketing manager at 50+ person company with complex needs
**Why HubSpot:** All-in-one platform, mature company with budget
**Pain point HubSpot solves:** Unified platform for complex marketing + CRM

### Simplified Customers
**Profile:** Solo creator, freelancer, or small agency (1-5 people)
**Why Simplified:** Fast content production across channels
**Pain point Simplified solves:** Content creation speed for social/marketing

### Marky Customers
**Profile:** Solo founder or small business (<10 people)
**Why Marky:** Simple, affordable social media posting
**Pain point Marky solves:** Consistent social media presence

### SteelWyre Ideal Customers
**Profile:** Founder/CEO or first marketer at bootstrapped startup (1-20 people)
**Why SteelWyre:** Need marketing strategy + execution without hiring; can't afford HubSpot
**Pain point SteelWyre solves:** Marketing execution bandwidth gap

## Red Flags: When to Use Each Competitor

**Use Jasper if:**
- You have an existing marketing process and just need content creation
- You have a team to execute content across channels
- You don't need strategic planning

**Use Copy.ai if:**
- Sales/prospecting is your primary focus
- You need GTM automation workflows
- You have enterprise budget

**Use HubSpot if:**
- You have a complex sales cycle requiring CRM + marketing integration
- You have budget ($890+/mo minimum)
- You need advanced attribution

**Use Simplified if:**
- You only need social media content
- You want visual design + video capabilities
- You're a solo creator or small agency

**Use Marky if:**
- You only need social media posting
- You're a small business wanting simple, affordable tool
- You don't need growth path or analytics

**Use SteelWyre if:** (Unique selling situations)
- You need both strategy generation AND execution
- You're bootstrapped with limited budget
- You don't want to hire a marketing team
- You want AI agents actually doing the work
- You're a founder needing full-cycle marketing solution

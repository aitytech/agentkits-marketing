## Three Critical Risks

### Risk #1: Starter Tier Unit Economics (HIGH)
- $49/month revenue vs. ~$45/month COGS = ~$4 gross margin
- If churn >5%/month or upgrade rate <40%, business model breaks
- **Action:** Calculate actual COGS immediately; be prepared to raise Starter to $69

### Risk #2: 46-Channel Breadth Perception (HIGH)
- Buyers don't trust generalists; they trust specialists
- Messaging "46 channels" makes SteelWyre sound unfocused
- Jasper will own content; Copy.ai will own workflows; SteelWyre = middle
- **Action:** Focus messaging on top 5-6 channels; de-emphasize breadth

### Risk #3: Defensibility Window Closes (MEDIUM)
- Jasper's execution layer: Q3 2026 (3-6 months away)
- Copy.ai's strategy module: Q4 2026 (6-9 months away)
- HubSpot's strategy AI: Q2 2027 (12-18 months away)
- **Action:** Establish market dominance + customer lock-in before Q3 2026

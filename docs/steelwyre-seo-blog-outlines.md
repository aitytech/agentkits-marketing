**Target keyword:** `ai marketing agent`
**Est. monthly searches:** 600-900
**Difficulty:** Low (emerging category)
**Intent:** Educational → brand authority

**Outline:**

1. **Hook** — "You've used ChatGPT to write a campaign brief. You've used Jasper to write ad copy. Neither one ran the campaign. Here's why."
2. **What is an AI marketing agent?** (vs. AI assistant vs. AI content tool)
   - Definition: autonomous software that takes actions, not just generates outputs
   - Analogy: the difference between a GPS giving directions and a self-driving car
3. **The 3 types of AI in marketing today**
   - Type 1: Generators (ChatGPT, Jasper, Copy.ai) — output text on demand
   - Type 2: Assistants (HubSpot Breeze, etc.) — suggestions within existing workflows
   - Type 3: Agents — plan, execute, monitor, and adjust campaigns autonomously
4. **What AI agents can actually do in marketing (2026)**
   - Build campaign strategy from goals
   - Create and publish content across platforms
   - Monitor performance and adjust in real time
   - Reallocate budget based on results
5. **What they still can't do** (be honest; builds trust)
   - Brand voice nuance without sufficient training data
   - High-stakes creative decisions
   - Relationship-based sales
6. **Who benefits most from AI marketing agents?**
   - Founders/SMBs without a marketing team
   - Agencies looking to increase margin
   - Solo marketers managing too many channels

- **Meta description:** 150-160 chars, includes keyword + CTA

- **CTA placement:** above the fold (intro), middle (contextual), bottom (strong)

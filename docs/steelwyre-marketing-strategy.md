## 2. Core Positioning

### Primary Position
> **"The AI that doesn't just write your marketing — it runs it."**

SteelWyre owns the **"doing gap"** — the space between AI content generation (Jasper, Copy.ai) and actual campaign execution. Every competitor stops at the output. SteelWyre closes the loop.

### Secondary Position (paid / SMB channels)
> **"What used to cost $4K/mo in agency fees. Now automated."**

### Positioning Matrix

## 3. Target Personas

### Persona 1 — "Scrappy Sam" (Primary)
- **Who:** Solo founder / small business owner, 1-5 employees
- **Budget:** $500-2,000/mo total marketing budget
- **Pain:** Knows they need marketing; can't afford an agency or full-time hire
- **Goal:** Grow revenue without growing headcount
- **Trigger:** Just got burned by an agency or tried Jasper and still had to "do everything"
- **Message that lands:** "Your competitor has a 10-person marketing team. You have SteelWyre."

### Persona 2 — "Agency Alex" (Secondary)
- **Who:** Boutique agency owner, 2-20 employees
- **Budget:** Looking to increase margin per client
- **Pain:** Staff time consumed by repetitive execution tasks
- **Goal:** Handle more clients without hiring
- **Trigger:** Lost a client to a cheaper competitor or struggling to scale
- **Message that lands:** "SteelWyre makes your agency 3x more profitable per client."

### Persona 3 — "Startup Felix" (Tertiary)
- **Who:** Technical founder at pre-seed/seed stage
- **Budget:** Limited runway; needs proof of traction
- **Pain:** Building product + doing all marketing = impossible
- **Goal:** Get to product-market fit signal without burning cash on marketing
- **Trigger:** Raised a small round and needs growth without a marketing hire
- **Message that lands:** "Enterprise marketing on a startup budget."

## 5. Channel Strategy

### Priority Stack (90-Day Focus)

| Priority | Channel | Why | Investment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SEO + Content | Compounding; own "AI marketing automation" category | Time + $300/mo tools |

### Channels to Defer (Post-90 Days)
- YouTube (high-value but takes 6+ months to compound)
- LinkedIn Ads (CPM too high for current stage)
- TikTok Ads (high creative cost for B2B)
- PR (low conversion without existing brand)

## 12. The One Unlock

**SteelWyre needs one undeniable proof point.**

A specific, named, verifiable customer result:
*"[Company X] replaced their $4K/mo agency. ROAS went from 2.1x to 4.7x in 60 days."*

Without this, every channel is pushing a claim. With this, every channel becomes a distribution vehicle for proof. Collecting and publishing this case study is the **single highest-leverage marketing task** in Month 1.

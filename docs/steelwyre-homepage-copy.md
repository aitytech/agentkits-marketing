## 8. FAQ SECTION

**Section Label:** Answers Before You Have to Ask

---

**Q1: How is this different from just using ChatGPT or Jasper?**

ChatGPT and Jasper generate content. That's valuable. But they stop there. You still have to take that content, format it for each platform, upload it to your ad manager, set up the targeting, launch it, monitor it, and optimize it when it plateaus. SteelWyre does all of that. The content creation is one piece. The execution, integration, and optimization are what actually move your numbers.

**Q2: Do I need technical skills or a developer to set this up?**

No. Connecting your accounts takes 5-10 minutes using guided OAuth flows — the same "click to connect" process you've done with every other SaaS tool. No API keys, no developer, no code. If you can use Shopify or HubSpot, you can use SteelWyre.

**Q3: Will I still have control over what goes live?**

Yes, completely. By default, SteelWyre generates and queues everything for your approval before anything publishes. You review the strategy, approve the copy, and confirm the launch. You control the throttle.

**Q4: What if my business is niche? Will the AI understand my industry?**

SteelWyre doesn't use generic templates. During onboarding, Indigo agents analyze your existing content, ads, and audience data to build a foundation specific to your business. The more connected accounts and historical data you bring in, the more accurately the system understands your customers.

**Q5: How is this better than hiring a marketing agency?**

The average small business pays $3,000-$6,000/month for an agency retainer. For the same output — campaigns planned, content created, ads launched, performance tracked — SteelWyre Operator costs $249/month. You lose the human relationship. You gain speed, consistency, and predictability. For most SMBs, that's the right trade.
